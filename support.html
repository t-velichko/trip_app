Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your device and the app works offline.

How do I restore Plan & Travel Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

What happens to my trips if I stay on the free version?

Nothing is hidden or deleted. The free version limits how many trips and items you can add; everything you already have stays yours.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Public rates are downloaded when you are online. The rate is saved with each amount when you enter it, so a payment made in August keeps the value it had in August.

Can I move my data to another device?

Your trips are stored on the device and are included in its regular backups. Pro users can also export a trip to PDF or CSV.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
