Privacy Policy

Plan & Travel · Leleka Labs · Last updated 14 October 2026

Plan & Travel is built to keep your trips on your device. We do not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (trips, plans, bookings, expenses, budgets, checklists and the photos you attach) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Your data may be included in your device's own backups if you have backups turned on; those are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy terms, not ours.

Exchange rates

To convert currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains only a currency code, such as EUR. It contains nothing you have entered and nothing that identifies you. Like any website, that service receives your device's IP address when the request is made. If the request fails, the app keeps working with the rates it already has.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to attach receipts, tickets and documents. Photos are stored only on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to lock your trip documents, such as passport pages. Biometric data is handled by your device and is never available to the app.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set on checklist items. They are scheduled on your device; no server is involved.

Each permission is optional. The rest of the app works without it.

Purchases

Plan & Travel Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details or your identity.

Exports

When you export a trip to PDF or CSV, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip in the app to remove it and its attachments. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
